Sleep and Recovery: How Rest Supports Addiction Treatment

In today's fast-paced world, sleep is often overlooked. It's important to note that rest plays a crucial role in recovery, and this comprehensive guide will delve into why.

Why sleep matters during treatment

Roughly 70% of people entering treatment report insomnia, and poor sleep raises relapse risk by 40%. Sleep problems can persist for 6 to 12 months after detox, and the anxiety and depression that often come with early recovery make rest harder.
